# Transition Plan (Unclassified)

Adoption Path
- Pilot the prototype on unclassified data with G‑3/G‑4/G‑8 users.
- Create SOPs for data refresh, scenario runs, and approvals.
- Train key staff (OpsO/MaintO/Comptroller) with short playbooks.

Sustainment
- Hand off code/docs to command‑designated maintainers.
- Establish cadence for doctrine updates and embedding refresh.
- Version and archive Scenario Packs and metrics reports.

Scale‑Out
- Generalize data mappers to other units; maintain MOS‑scoped doctrine links.
- Optional: package ML models as Core ML/ONNX for embedded use or service calls.

Exit Criteria
- Prototype, paper, and training materials delivered.
- At least one scenario shows measurable improvement in timeliness and planning quality.
- Endorsement from G‑3/G‑4/G‑8 to move to broader pilot.
