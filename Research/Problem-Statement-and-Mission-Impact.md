# Problem Statement & Mission Impact (Unclassified)

Problem
Commands continuously balance readiness requirements, training obligations (TEEP), operational plans, and finite resources. Current tools provide fragmented snapshots. Staff must manually merge readiness, maintenance, funding, and training data with doctrine and commander guidance, creating delays, inconsistency, and blind spots to second‑ and third‑order effects.

Mission Impact
- Latent readiness risks go undetected until they degrade capability or disrupt TEEP.
- Decision latency increases during cross‑functional issues (maintenance + training + funding) that span G‑3/G‑4/G‑8.
- Inconsistent evidence trails and limited explainability reduce trust and repeatability.

ARCnet Approach
- Unify unclassified data into a Single Source of Truth (SSOT) and reason over it with doctrinally seeded agents.
- Predict maintenance/readiness dips and surface mitigation windows that preserve TEEP.
- Produce structured, evidence‑backed COAs with approvals at every gate.

Example Outcomes (measurable targets)
- Reduce time to assemble readiness + funds + TEEP picture from hours/days to minutes.
- Predict and mitigate ≥ 60% of readiness dips ≥ 2 weeks in advance.
- Provide ≥ 2 viable COAs per planning ask with quantified tradeoffs and zero/low TEEP degradation.
- Commander usefulness ratings ≥ 4/5 for clarity/actionability.
