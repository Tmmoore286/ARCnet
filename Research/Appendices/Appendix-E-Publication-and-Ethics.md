# Appendix E — Publication & Ethics (Unclassified)

Publication Plan
- Maintain unclassified content; remove operational specifics; aggregate examples and synthetic data as needed.
- Target venues: service journals, applied AI venues appropriate for government work.
- Include methods, metrics, error analysis, and limitations; provide scenario descriptions and rubrics.

Ethical Considerations
- Human‑in‑the‑loop authority preserved; AI is advisory.
- Avoid over‑automation; require approvals at gates; log dissent and alternatives.
- Equity and bias safeguards in selection; diversity penalties; shop coverage requirements.

Data Handling
- No PII; no controlled/classified data; redaction applied upfront.
- Store unclassified prototypes and logs in approved repositories.
