# Use Case — Readiness Optimization Under TEEP Constraints (Unclassified)

Primary Question
“What is my current readiness and funding posture, and how can I increase readiness without degrading my approved TEEP?”

Scope
- Unit: 1st MLG (generalizable to other echelons/commands).
- Domains: maintenance readiness, training/TEEP, personnel availability (high level), funding posture.
- Constraints: TEEP non‑degradation (or explicit offsets), classification unclassified, human‑in‑the‑loop approvals at gates.

Inputs (unclassified)
- Readiness snapshots and indicators (G‑3/G‑4).
- Maintenance status and logs (G‑4).
- Historical budget information, status of funds, future budgets (G‑8).
- Training requirements and TEEP schedules (G‑3).
- Commander’s intent, end‑state, constraints, and acceptance criteria.

Outputs
- Factual status summary (readiness/funding/TEEP) grounded in SSOT.
- Forecast of readiness/maintenance risks and mitigation windows.
- 2–4 COAs with quantified readiness deltas, cost, TEEP impacts, risks, and evidence citations.
- Tasking outline for approved COA; exportable artifacts.

Assumptions
- All data remains unclassified and is command‑approved for use.
- No PII; redact as needed; synthetic or derived data permitted when required.
- LLM usage limited to OpenAI; no enterprise connectors/MCP in MVP prototype.
