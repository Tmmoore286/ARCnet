# Executive Summary (Unclassified)

The Adaptive Reasoning and Collaboration Network (ARCnet) delivers a commander‑in‑control, AI‑enabled decision environment that unifies unit readiness, training requirements (TEEP), operational commitments, and funding posture into a single, explainable workflow. For 1st MLG (and any Marine Air‑Ground Task Force or supporting establishment), ARCnet accelerates staff processes by combining:

- A Single Source of Truth (SSOT) for unclassified readiness, maintenance, training/TEEP, and budget data (owned by G‑3/G‑4/G‑8).
- Doctrinally seeded, MOS‑scoped AI agents that reason transparently with human‑in‑the‑loop approvals.
- Machine learning models for maintenance and readiness forecasting to surface bottlenecks before they degrade capability.
- A COA generator/evaluator that proposes options, quantifies impacts, and preserves commander authority through approvals at every gate.

Research objectives:
- A working, unclassified prototype focused on “Readiness Optimization Under TEEP Constraints,” with dashboards, explainable checkpoints, and exportable tasking.
- ML maintenance/readiness forecasting (pilot) integrated into the reasoning loop and measured against rubric‑based accuracy and timeliness.
- A publishable paper documenting methods, results, and applicability to other USMC commands.

Operational impact: faster, evidence‑based decisions; fewer readiness dips; reduced manual reconciliation across staff sections; and a repeatable, auditable process that scales beyond 1st MLG.
